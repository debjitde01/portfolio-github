Academic Biography

About Dr. Debjit De

I am an energetic bioinformaticist and academician with grounded competencies across the molecular wet-lab and advanced computational genomics.

My research journey bridges laboratory protocols (DNA extraction, PCR, microbial culturing, genotoxicity evaluation) with cutting-edge computational infrastructure. I specialize in 16S rRNA amplicon and shotgun metagenomic sequencing, high-dimensional microbial ecology metrics (QIIME 2, DADA2, MetaPhlAn, HUMAnN2), and statistical machine learning for disease group stratification.

Currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Bioinformatics, School of Bio-Engineering and Bio-Sciences, Lovely Professional University (LPU), and having completed my doctoral thesis at Jadavpur University and served at the prestigious National Institute of Biomedical Genomics (NIBMG) and Swami Vivekananda University, I lead investigative pipelines unravelling microbiome shifts in Type 2 Diabetes, SARS-CoV-2 variant infections, maternal preterm birth markers, and environmental metagenomics.
